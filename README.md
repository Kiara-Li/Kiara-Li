I am a communication designer and creative technologist at Parsons School of Design. I build research-driven interfaces, archives, and experiments that ask how technology shapes what people notice, remember, and trust.

My current work moves between **AI and information systems**, **participatory archives**, **embodied interaction**, and **urban multispecies research**. I am especially interested in making invisible systems—classification, recommendation, translation, and data collection—available for people to inspect and question.

## Selected work

### [The Feed as Archive](https://kiara-li.github.io/THE_FEED_AS_ARCHIVE/) ↗

`information environment` `algorithmic visibility` `research archive`

A research-based web archive about how search engines, social feeds, moderation systems, and AI summaries determine what becomes visible, legitimate, or absent. It brings theoretical texts, documented cases, and a first-person feed audit into one navigable argument.

**[Live site](https://kiara-li.github.io/THE_FEED_AS_ARCHIVE/)** · **[Repository](https://github.com/Kiara-Li/THE_FEED_AS_ARCHIVE)**

---

### [Sensing Suspicion](https://github.com/Kiara-Li/Sensing-Suspicion)

`NLP` `RoBERTa` `model interpretation` `data visualization`

A text-classification experiment trained on Reddit narratives to recognize linguistic patterns associated with unsettling or suspicious situations. The interface exposes sentence-level scores, a narrative tension curve, and highlighted passages so the model's judgment can be examined rather than treated as a black box.

**[Repository](https://github.com/Kiara-Li/Sensing-Suspicion)** · **[Model](https://huggingface.co/KiaraLi2025/creepy-roberta/tree/main)**

---

### [Clipboard Forest](https://github.com/Kiara-Li/clipboard-tracker)

`browser extension` `information provenance` `personal data visualization`

A Chrome extension that turns copied fragments into a growing geometric forest. Source type determines each tree's form, text length determines its height, and every fragment keeps a path back to where it came from—making everyday information-gathering visible over time.

**[Repository](https://github.com/Kiara-Li/clipboard-tracker)**

---

### [TRACE / Living Archive](https://kiara-li.github.io/thesis-living-archive/trace/) ↗

`participatory archive` `bilingual interface` `research method`

A bilingual participatory tool for drawing the time people have spent in different cities and recording the animals and plants they remember there. Each response can be exported as an image or PDF while preserving structured trace data for later research and visualization.

**[Try TRACE](https://kiara-li.github.io/thesis-living-archive/trace/)** · **[Living Archive](https://kiara-li.github.io/thesis-living-archive/)** · **[Repository](https://github.com/Kiara-Li/thesis-living-archive)**

---

### [Pigeon Viewpoint Experiment](https://kiara-li.github.io/prototype1/) ↗

`computer vision` `embodied interaction` `p5.js` `MediaPipe`

An embodied web experiment that translates head angle into vertical navigation through a book of pigeon observations. Related modes use hand gestures to frame and magnify photographs and let participants attach recorded sounds to individual birds.

**[Live prototype](https://kiara-li.github.io/prototype1/)** · **[Repository](https://github.com/Kiara-Li/prototype1)**

---

### [Packed Like Sardines](https://packed-like-sardines.vercel.app/) ↗

`generative AI` `participatory storytelling` `emotional interface`

An interactive archive for urban commuters to compress a stressful experience into a digital sardine can. Gemini turns the account into a short satirical ingredient list; participants can browse other cans and release responses back into a shared subway environment.

**[Live site](https://packed-like-sardines.vercel.app/)** · **[Repository](https://github.com/Kiara-Li/packed-like-sardines)**

---

### [Exam Sprint](https://kiara-li.github.io/Kiara-Li-exam-sprint-overlay/) ↗

`adaptive learning` `interaction design` `prototyping`

A time-aware study layer for a Modern Art History learning interface. It turns an exam deadline into a study plan, supports swipe-to-save and review queues, reallocates time as chapters are completed, and records session events for prototype testing.

**[Live prototype](https://kiara-li.github.io/Kiara-Li-exam-sprint-overlay/)** · **[Repository](https://github.com/Kiara-Li/Kiara-Li-exam-sprint-overlay)**
